# Munim launch film: the Cup cut — "A day at Cobnut Bakehouse"

**Length:** 2:55 (175.0 s), scene windows as the spec table. The Cup T&C allows strictly under 3:00.
**Narration:** 365 words (2.1 words a second across the cut). No line goes faster than 2.9 words a second inside its window.
**Voice:** a local Kokoro narrator through `npx hyperframes tts`. The spike (Task 4) auditioned `bm_george` and `bf_emma` on lines 3.1–3.4; the choice is **`bf_emma`**. She says every line inside its window at natural speed (2.9, 5.3, 3.1 and 1.9 s against windows of 3.4, 6.0, 4.0 and 2.6 s). George runs 3.6 s and 6.5 s on 3.1 and 3.2, past their windows, and would need about 10% more speed across the cut. The George mix is kept for the founder to compare (`assets/audio/mix-spike-bm_george.wav`).
**Voice direction:** plain, confident and warm. Short sentences, said like someone who runs a shop. No hype, and no upward lilt on the ends of lines.
**Captions:** the "Caption" column word for word, in one caption band at the bottom of the frame. **Stickers** are the stamped headline captions, at most one on screen at a time. When a sticker or stamp carries a line word for word (3.4, 10.1), that line skips the caption band, so it is not shown twice.
**TTS text:** what Kokoro is fed. Numbers are written as words, and names are respelt only where espeak's en-gb phonemes get them wrong. "Munim" comes out "MEW-nim", so it is respelt "Moo-neem". "Nandini", "Imran" and "Kavya" get the wrong stress, so they become "Nun-dee-nee", "Im-raan" and "Kahv-yah". "Zoya" and "Farah" are already right, so they keep their spelling. The captions keep the real spellings.
**Times are provisional:** they are seconds from the start of the cut. The real voice durations replace them, and `assets/js/timing-cup.js` is the clock.

Munim is the subject of every line. Cobnut Bakehouse is "a business that runs on Munim". Every figure on screen comes from `assets/js/fixture.js`.
