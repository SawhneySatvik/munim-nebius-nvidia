  "content derived from or relating to" the entry. That one has no end date.
- `FACT` The T&C "shall prevail" over promotional materials, and the organiser may amend the T&C
  without notice. A dated snapshot is kept in this folder.
- `INFERENCE` (not legal advice): it is a matching right, not a veto. Selling non-exclusive SaaS
  subscriptions looks unaffected. A sale of the IP, an exclusive licence, and possibly an acquisition
  are what it catches, for 6 months. Sending from a personal address rather than a work one is your call.
