# FalkorDB Graph Hacks — rules and schedule (snapshot)

Fetched 15 Oct 2026 from https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/falkordb/rules and `/schedule`. The quotes below are as
the fetch tool returned them. Before submitting, re-read the live pages (gotcha 19).

**Schedule.** Hacking begins "Oct 15, 12:01 AM IST". Submissions close "Oct 18, 11:59 PM IST". No results date is
given.

**Rules (numbered as on the page):**
- **2.** "Required technology: FalkorDB must be the primary graph database in your project, and it must support a
  central part of what the product does."
- **3.** "The graph has to do real work. A visualisation drawn on top of ordinary storage does not qualify: if the
  product still works once FalkorDB is taken out, the graph is decoration rather than the thing the product runs on."
- **4.** "Run FalkorDB however suits you. Docker, a self-hosted instance, and FalkorDB Cloud are all accepted, and you
  may use any language, framework, frontend, or deployment platform alongside it."
- **7.** "The hackathon runs from October 15, 12:01 AM IST to October 18, 11:59 PM IST. Submissions close at the end
  of that window."
- **8.** "The main coding and design work must begin after the hackathon starts. You may discuss ideas, create notes,
  sketch the graph model, or prepare diagrams beforehand."
- **10.** The required deliverables:
  - a public source-code repository;
  - a README with setup instructions and the graph data model;
  - the Cypher queries or graph algorithms the product depends on;
  - a demo video showing the working project;
  - a live deployment or complete local setup instructions;
  - the track name(s).

**Tracks:**
- Agents That Act on Connected Data
- Agent Memory and Coordination
- Company Brain

**Team.** Solo, or up to 4 members; each person may be in one team only.

**Ledger.** R12 (separate product, coded from 15 Oct) is on record. R56 G-3: the founder owns the eligibility call
for a Munim-based entry.
